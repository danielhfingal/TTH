# Coaxial Implementation – From Factory to Reality

## Fabrication Process
1. Inner pipe: HDPE/composite extrusion with embedded fiber-optic/TENG layers.
2. Outer liner: Reinforced concrete/steel segments.
3. Assembly: Inner inserted, annulus sealed (vacuum/gel optional).

## Deployment Process
- TBM continuous advance → robotic arms erect/align/seal segments.
- Pigging ports every 5km for maintenance.

## Electricity Cables Integration
- Central conductor + braided shield → same prefab as fluid coaxial.
- Added value: EMI shielding critical for AI racks; micro-capacitive harvest bonus.

## Challenges & Solutions
- Thermal expansion: Centralizers + flexible joints.
- Alignment: Laser guidance + Nexus real-time correction.
